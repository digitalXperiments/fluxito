Step 3 of 5

Make Fluxito yours.

Every business defines metrics differently. Tell your AI assistant exactly what your KPIs mean and give it the context it needs — then reuse pre-built templates to save hours of setup.

Templates
Browse the Fluxito library of pre-built dashboard recipes — Ecommerce overview, PPC performance, GTM audit, and more. Deploy one with a single click or ask your AI to set it up.
Find it under Insights → Templates.
KPI Library
Define the KPIs that matter to your business — name, description, formula, data source, and owner. Your AI reads this glossary automatically so every answer uses your definitions, not generic ones.
Example: CAC = "total paid marketing spend ÷ first-time customers, excluding refunds within 30 days." Source: BigQuery.finance.orders. Owner: Marketing team.
Find it under Knowledge → KPI Library.
Business Context
A plain-Markdown doc describing your industry, audience, seasonality, internal terminology, and any rules your AI should apply. Loaded on demand whenever you ask an analytics question.
Find it under Knowledge → Business Context.
These are optional — you can skip them and add them later. But the more context your AI has, the sharper its answers get.
