Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is MCP and why does my AI need it? +
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard that connects AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, or Antigravity to external tools. Without MCP, AI is just a chatbot that can only generate text. With {{ brand().name }} MCP, your AI gains direct, authenticated tools to audit your tags, inspect live network traffic, and stage changes in Tag Manager.
Do I need to know how to code to use {{ brand().name }} MCP? +
No. {{ brand().name }} MCP was built specifically for marketing directors, media buyers, and growth managers. You ask your AI questions in plain English, and it translates your marketing goals into precise Tag Manager and analytics actions.
Can my AI accidentally break my live website or tags? +
Never. Fluxito is strictly read-only by default. When you ask it to create or modify tags, it stages the changes inside an isolated Tag Manager draft workspace. Your live website is never altered until you click Approve.
Do I have to pay Fluxito token fees? +
No. {{ brand().name }} MCP is open source and connects directly to your existing AI subscriptions (Claude, ChatGPT Plus, Grok, or Antigravity). We never charge token markups.
