Meet Flux

A week of work, without the headcount.

This is what an experienced analytics person does. Flux does all of it — and asks before touching anything live.

MONThe briefing Overnight scan of every connected source. Anomalies explained, wins flagged, fixes drafted — in your inbox, Slack, or on the home screen.
TUEThe tracking plan Generates and versions your Solution Design Reference from what's actually live in GA4 and GTM — events, parameters, owners, gaps. Exports to Excel for the people who still want Excel.
WEDThe implementation Turns the plan into GTM tags, triggers and variables in a draft workspace. You review the diff; Flux publishes. No more clicking through the container by hand.
THUThe audit Live tag audit of your site against 25 platform rule books. Duplicate purchases, dead conversions, drift between plan and reality — caught before finance catches it.
FRIThe report Builds the dashboard, writes the narrative, schedules the recap. Paid, web, SEO and warehouse in one story — with a public link for the exec who won't log in.
ALWAYSThe watch Hourly anomaly monitors across GA4, Meta and Google Ads. Budget pacing, conversion health, competitor movement — Flux only pings you when it matters.
